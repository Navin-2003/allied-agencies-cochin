Our Legacy

Expert Water Engineering

Founded in 1973 in Thrippunithura, Allied Agencies Cochin stands as Kerala's premier consultant and supplier of water management solutions. We specialize in precision pumping systems for domestic wells, agricultural irrigation networks, and robust industrial fluid transmissions. Partnering with top-tier brands like Kirloskar, V-Guard, Texmo, and CRI, we deliver custom-engineered systems that optimize efficiency, sustainability, and longevity.
